Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ("the App", "we") handles your information. We built the App to be useful without collecting more than it needs.

What the App Does

The App provides an in-car style dashboard experience: a driving dashboard, a GPS-based speedometer, an AI-assisted repair helper, a VIN lookup tool, and a garage for your vehicles.

Information We Process

  • Location (GPS): used on your device to show speed and map-related features while you use them.
  • Camera and photos: if you use the AI repair feature, photos you take or select are processed only to analyze your car issue. We do not access your camera or photo library for any other purpose.
  • Vehicle details: information you enter, such as a VIN or the vehicles in your garage, is used to provide those features.

Subscriptions & Payments

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed and managed by Apple through your App Store account. We do not receive or store your payment card details. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store settings at any time.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use your camera or photos outside the repair feature.

Data Retention & Security

We keep data only as long as needed to provide the features you use, and we apply reasonable technical measures to protect it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version.
